# Film artwork and reuse audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-17  
**Result:** no third-party image was copied into the project. All 34 active artwork assets remain local, project-created SVG illustrations. Each is labeled as an original illustration, not a film poster or still. The active artwork's source and license statement are also stored in `lib/movies.ts` and displayed in film details.

An image being visible on a studio, archive, festival, distributor, or film database page is not itself a reuse license. The Azerbaijani Copyright Agency explains that copyright arises when a work is created, and the law reserves reproduction and public display rights to the right holder. The film archive pages cited below identify their assets as covers/posters and/or state that film rights belong to Azerbaijanfilm. None of the candidate image pages reviewed provides an explicit license for copying the image into this public website. Permission must be obtained from the relevant image/film rights holder before any of these candidates replace the original artwork.

## Current counts

- Authentic film images with verified permission integrated: **0**.
- Original local illustrations retained: **34**.

The original SVG assets are lightweight, served locally, lazy-loaded in cards, and rendered with preserved poster proportions. To add authentic imagery later, obtain written permission or a clear license from the specific rights holder, then record the exact asset source, attribution, and license in `lib/movies.ts` before replacing that film's illustration.

## YouTube video previews (2026-11-17)

For films with a matching upload on the verified Azərbaycanfilm channel, the homepage hero embeds the featured film and a card button mounts a preview for only the selected card. A single shared selection means no more than one card preview is mounted at a time; opening a details dialog closes that card preview. The video does not autoplay; YouTube renders and attributes its thumbnail inside its player, with YouTube branding and controls intact and an adjacent source link. The project does not request or store YouTube thumbnail URLs, screenshot, copy, cache, or alter thumbnails, and does not call the YouTube Data API. This is an embedded-video preview, not an assertion that the thumbnail has an independent poster license. YouTube requires clear attribution for displayed YouTube content and prohibits obscuring player attribution/controls; its embed rules also prohibit overlays over the player. The iframe is kept clear of overlays, uses `strict-origin-when-cross-origin` referrer policy, and has a responsive 16:9 viewport. The official player implementation does not require user authorization. See [YouTube Developer Policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies), [Required Minimum Functionality](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/required-minimum-functionality), and [YouTube copyright guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12361994?hl=en). The official Data API can return thumbnail metadata, but this project deliberately uses the supported player surface rather than extracting the image URL ([videos resource](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos)).

Official channel title and descriptions, film synopsis, and production/director credits were used to match the candidate videos. The channel upload is a rights-holder source for the video/player integration; it does **not** establish an independent license to republish its thumbnail as a standalone image.
